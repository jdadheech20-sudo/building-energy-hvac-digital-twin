# Project Context — AI-Powered Digital Twin for Sustainable Smart Campuses

## 1. Project Overview

**Project title:** AI-Powered Digital Twin Platform for Sustainable Smart Campuses  
**Domain:** Smart Cities, AI, Digital Twins, Predictive Analytics  
**Sustainable Development Goal:** SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities and Communities

We are building a B.Tech major project that uses AI and a digital twin to help campus administrators understand building energy usage, predict demand, detect abnormal consumption, and evaluate sustainability interventions.

## 4. Data Constraints

We do not currently have access to actual campus electricity bills or detailed campus sensor records.

We may use public building-energy datasets, campus metadata, historical weather data, and clearly labelled synthetic sensor data.

Potential public dataset: ASHRAE Great Energy Predictor III.

We must distinguish measured data, model predictions, and simulated scenarios. We must not present simulated energy savings as verified real-world savings.

## 5. Intended Research Contribution

We want the project to go beyond a standard dashboard or a basic combination of AI models.

Our proposed innovation is a **Trust-Aware, Data-Scarcity-Aware Campus Digital Twin**, featuring:

1. Cross-building or transfer-learning approaches for buildings with limited historical data.
2. Prediction reliability checks that estimate uncertainty and flag unreliable predictions.
3. Data-acquisition guidance that identifies which additional measurements would most improve prediction quality.
4. Budget-constrained sustainability planning that ranks interventions using estimated savings, cost, comfort constraints, and uncertainty.
5. An interactive campus twin that visualizes building-level forecasts, anomalies, and what-if scenarios.

A multi-agent architecture may be used if it adds measurable value. Potential agents include an Energy Forecasting Agent, Data Quality and Trust Agent, Sustainability Planning Agent, and Explanation Agent.
